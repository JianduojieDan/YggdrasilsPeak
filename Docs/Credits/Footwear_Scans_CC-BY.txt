Footwear scans used on the Chapter 1 cast (Chomolungma). Downloaded 2026-11-14 without login.
Every model below is licensed Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Changes made for the game (all of them): mesh welded, extra pieces removed, decimated to ~9k triangles, re-oriented,
scaled and reshaped to each character's foot, skinned to the foot/ball bones, mirrored for the right foot,
texture copied (Tools/Blender/garments.py, make_scan_boots).
Trademarks are NOT licensed by CC BY ("Patent and trademark rights are not licensed under this Public License"):
brand logos/wordmarks visible on the scans should be painted out before a commercial release.
