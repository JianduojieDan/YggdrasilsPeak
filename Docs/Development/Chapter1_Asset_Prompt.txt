Create realistic 3D game assets for a first-person game set on the real Everest trek in Nepal (the Khumbu valley, Dingboche village and the Thukla pass, 4,400 to 4,850 m), present day, spring. The look is photoreal and lived-in: used, faded, dusty, weathered gear, never brand-new or stylised. All people are Sherpa or Nepali locals and foreign trekkers.

Rules for every asset:
- Real-world scale in metres. One asset per file, GLB or FBX.
- PBR textures (base colour, normal, roughness, metallic where there is metal), with NO baked lighting or shadows in the base colour. 2K textures for large or hero assets, 1K for small props.
- Clean, game-ready topology: small props 1-5k triangles, medium props 5-15k, tents 15-30k, characters 40-60k.
- No logos, no text, no brand look-alikes.
- Characters and clothing in A-pose (arms about 45 degrees down), feet on the ground, rigged for a standard humanoid skeleton. Clothing as separate pieces where possible.

CAMP
