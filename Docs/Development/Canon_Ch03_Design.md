## 4. Mechanics, people, sound, failure

### 4.1 Teaching ladder

| Mechanic | Introduce | Develop | Twist | Conclude |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Crampons (HZ9) | strap at Crampon Point | steps bite on ice | — | worn to Camp 1 |
| Clipping (CL6, key anchors only) | first anchor | ladder and zone ends | the queue and the old track tempt you off | the bridge, or the fall |
| Ladders (CL3) | Ch2 practice / ladder 1, Nima holds | ladder 3 you lead, 4 no help | **ladder 2: you hold Elena's line** (ST4) | ladder 5 alone mid-span; ladder 6 Nima does not wait |

One teacher (Nima), one line at the need, three separate places. Periodic breathing develops (Ch2 → Ch3) without a
teacher. Systems used: HZ1 (gate), CL1-3, CL6, HZ2-4, HZ6, HZ9, NP1, ST1-4, ST6, BD5-8, BD10, CP1, CP2. Gloves wait for Ch6.

### 4.2 People

Nima leads, teaches, lets go, and prays. Elena is in your hands. Ji-woo films the fall (it returns in Ch5). Marek is the
clock. Pemba takes Elena down if asked. Pasang is a hand and a voice. Two Icefall Doctors and two descending Sherpas are
the mountain's workers (one recoloured faceless model). Dr. Hale is on the radio at 03:15, 06:30 and 19:00. Sherpas are
the experts in every scene; no client saves Nima.

### 4.3 Sound and weather (the director picks every sound by ear)

Clear, still, −20 °C at 03:00; realms off. **Sound before sight:** juniper crackle, a creak inside each zone, the
strangers' shout, Elena's breath at ladder 2, the crack 0.4 s before the fall, Nima's voice behind you at the light.
**Beds:** Icefall groans, wind in the slots, crampons on aluminium, carabiner gates. **Silence as a beat:** the Cwm's
noise fading; the sleeping camp at 03:00. **Music:** one earned cue at the Cwm after 4 km of ice, deep and fateful;
none at the fall or the end.

### 4.4 Failure and recovery

| What goes wrong | What happens | Lasting mark |
|---|---|---|
| Ladder slip, clipped | hang, pull up (CL3) | cold, health −, Nima: *"Again. Slower."*, journal line |

| Late (D1 or slow) | serac clock ×1.7, the fall ahead, a detour | the late day |
| The radio lie | nothing tonight | Hale, in person, Ch5 |

## 8. Cultural notes [CC]

- The altar's name (*lhapso* not found in sources) and a juniper offering before the Icefall: ask.
- Clockwise round the altar; asking Miyolangsangma for safe passage: likely right.
- *Lha gyalo* at the top of the Icefall: natural there? *Om mani padme hum* when afraid at night: likely right.
- *Dai*, *bhai*, *bistarai* are Nepali; what Sherpas say among themselves: ask.
- The Doctor names nobody: the safe line avoids the dead man's name.
- No belief about lights at night is invented; "the mountain has a root" is one grandfather's story.
- The serac fall hurts nobody: no rescue, no echo of the April 2014 avalanche that killed 16 Nepali workers.
- Carry bonus, the 8 kg rubbish rule, tea before an Icefall start, Icefall Doctors as real job with fictional people: check.
