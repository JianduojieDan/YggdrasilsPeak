# 2026-11-13 (overnight) — menus v2

The look of every menu was redone ("industrial level"), and the menu bugs from the 2026-11-12 play-test were fixed.
Everything was checked with screenshots of the real game (`Tools/UI/menu_shots.ps1`). Nothing here has been judged by the
player yet.

## The design language (YggUIStyle.h)
- A frame of hairlines on every screen. The header shows `YGGDRASIL'S PEAK / <where you are>` with a place on the right;
  the footer shows a quiet line on the left and key caps on the right.
- Film grain and a vignette over the whole screen. On the mountain, the world behind the menu goes soft (background blur).
- Numbered options. The chosen one sits in a band of soft light with an accent bar: gold, or ember on the death screen.
- The World Tree emblem, drawn in vector lines (`YggEmblem`): a ring with nine beads for the realms, the mountain, and the
  tree rising from the summit with its roots in the rock. It draws itself on when it appears. On the loading screen, a
  light runs round its ring while the world loads.
- Prompts switch to gamepad buttons (A, B, D-pad, LB RB) while a pad is used.

## Bugs fixed
- **#62** The hover description no longer moves the menu: it has a fixed place. The Upscaling row no longer runs off the
  edge, because choices are now selectors. The list also no longer jumps between tabs.
- **#64** After the start menu the player could not walk or look. The cause: the start menu's UI-only input mode lives on
  the game viewport and survives the level change. Game input is now set when the climb starts. Checked in the game:
  start menu → New expedition → P opens the pause menu.
- **#65** Field of view is now 90–110°, the first row of the Game tab.
- **#63** (part) New detail options: Shadows, View distance, Effects and Textures. "Preset" follows the Quality tier; the
  automatic quality can still step down. DLSS still needs NVIDIA's plugin, and its licence is yours to accept.
- The backpack card title no longer runs off the paper ("Father's compass").
