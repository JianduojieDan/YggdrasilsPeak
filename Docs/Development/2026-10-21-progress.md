# 2026-10-21 progress

## Terrain
- Landscape rebuilt at 2x resolution (~17 m vertex spacing) with baked noise (fBm + ridged gullies, stronger on steep ground). `ALandscapeRebuilder` rebuilds automatically when a noise value changes.
- Snow is matte instead of metallic (roughness 0.82-0.92, specular 0.3) and has stronger wind-blown ridges.
- Rock displacement biased upward, so the player no longer floats above deep rock dips. The hidden old landscape no longer has collision or shadows.
- PCG rock scatter is back: about 4,500 rocks with collision; small rocks are culled beyond 800 m.

## Player
- First-person camera at eye level, gently following the head animation (`UFirstPersonBodyComponent`).
- The player's own body is visible with the head hidden. A shadow-only copy of the body keeps a full shadow.
- The mouse-wheel zoom no longer pulls the camera back to third person.

## Route
- Nine route landmarks (`ARouteLandmark`) placed at their real positions: Dingboche, Gorak Shep, Everest Base Camp, Khumbu Icefall, Camps 1-3, South Col, Summit.

## Gameplay (Phase 3)
- Oxygen stat driven by altitude and effort, with bottled oxygen. Cold exposure grows with altitude. Walking uphill costs stamina and speed.
- Camps act as shelter and as checkpoints. Reaching the summit is tracked, and returning to Base Camp completes the expedition. After death the player respawns at the last camp.
- HUD shows an altimeter and an oxygen readout, plus messages for camps, the summit, deaths and the finish. Low oxygen also shows as a screen effect.
- Console commands: `ygg.Expedition.Go <0-8>`, `ygg.Survival.Bottle`, `ygg.Survival.LogEvery <s>`.

## Performance
- Standalone game at 1080p runs at about 120-127 FPS (render thread about 8 ms).
